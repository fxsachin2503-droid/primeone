FAQ

Before you participate.

What is the PrimeHarbour term?

The public site presents a one-month capital term. The displayed percentage only applies where the applicable written agreement provides for it and should be read with the eligibility conditions.

How is the 40% figure shown?

The calculator currently treats the website percentage as a capital-based illustrative figure for the one-month term. The actual contractual treatment is determined by the applicable written agreement.

How does the loss-refund policy work?

The displayed percentage is a policy percentage for qualifying losses where the written agreement provides for it. Eligibility, limits, exclusions and timing are determined by that agreement.

Are the monthly slides guaranteed?

No. Monthly slides are presentation components for published figures and should be populated only with figures supported by the platform's records.

What happens after I submit the acknowledgement?

The request is stored by the website. Where email is configured, an acknowledgement image and request details are emailed to the configured receiver. The site then directs the requester to the configured official Discord route.

Who are the founders?

PrimeHarbour is presented as being developed by Sachin Chaudhary and Abhitesh Chaudhary.
