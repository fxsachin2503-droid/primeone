THE COMPLETE ROUTE

Five moves.
One clear path.

Keep every important step inside the published route. The Discord server is a communication and review channel; the applicable written agreement remains the reference for final terms and eligibility.

01 · WEBSITE

Read first.

Review the one-month term, displayed percentages, risk information, eligibility conditions and agreement information before entering Discord.

Start from PrimeHarbour
02 · DISCORD

Join the server.

Use the official published invite and confirm the server identity before interacting with channels, bots or members.

Verify before you continue
03 · TICKET

Open your ticket.

Use the designated ticket/support channel for participation, account, agreement, payment or settlement questions.

Keep the conversation in-ticket
04 · REVIEW

Send the details.

Provide only the information reasonably requested for your review. Check your name, email and requested amount before sending.

No passwords or OTPs

Read & decide.

Agreement before payment

Keep everything.

Keep your own copies
WHERE TO OPEN IT

Find the
ticket route.

Once you enter the official server, look for the channel or panel designated for support, participation or open-ticket requests. Use that route instead of sending investment or payment details to random members by direct message.

  • Open the ticket from the server's designated ticket panel.
  • Choose the category that matches your request.
  • Complete the ticket form or message shown by the server.
  • Wait for a response inside the ticket thread.

    SERVER & PARTICIPATION RULES

    Simple rules.
    Protect your account.

    These website rules are a practical safety layer. The applicable written agreement and Discord server rules control where they differ.

    Use official routes only.

    Use the invite and ticket route published by PrimeHarbour. Treat unsolicited DMs and alternative payment instructions as unverified.

    Never share credentials.

    No legitimate review step should require your Discord password, email password, OTP, 2FA backup code, private key or seed phrase.

    Agreement before payment.

    Review the applicable written agreement, eligibility conditions and payment instructions before sending funds.

    Keep a record.

    Save your request ID, ticket history, agreement version, payment receipt and official confirmation.

    Understand the risk.

    Trading and market activity can result in losses. Website examples and displayed percentages are not a guarantee of an outcome.

    Refund terms are conditional.

    Any loss-refund treatment applies only where the applicable written agreement provides for it and its eligibility conditions are met.
