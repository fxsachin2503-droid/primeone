PRICING & REVIEW

Ask inside
the ticket.

The website can explain the structure and displayed figures, while the ticket provides a single place to clarify the current amount, applicable terms and the next step. Do not treat a Discord message by itself as a substitute for the written agreement.

Current amount

Check the Investment Desk for the figures currently displayed on the website. If you need clarification about the amount applicable to your request, ask inside the official ticket.

One-month term

The public site presents a one-month capital term. The exact contractual treatment is governed by the applicable written agreement and eligibility conditions.

Agreement first

Before payment or participation, read the applicable written agreement and confirm the amount, conditions, eligibility and settlement treatment.

No random DMs

Keep account, agreement, payment and settlement questions inside the designated official route. Do not follow payment changes or urgent requests from unrelated accounts.

Security rule

PrimeHarbour should never ask for your Discord password, OTP, private key or seed phrase. If a message looks unexpected or asks you to move the conversation to an unrelated account, stop and verify through the official route.
